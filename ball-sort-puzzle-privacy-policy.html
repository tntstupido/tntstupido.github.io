Privacy Policy for Ball Sort: Offline Puzzles

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Developer: Mladen Stojanovic
Contact: mladenstojanovicpo@gmail.com

Ball Sort: Offline Puzzles ("the App") is a color-sorting puzzle game for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the App, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

Short version: the App does not require an account. Game progress and settings are stored on your device. Native builds may use Firebase Analytics and Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), with Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising consent where required. Where the Remove Ads purchase is available, it is handled by the Apple App Store on iOS or Google Play on Android.

1. Information stored locally

The App stores the information needed to provide gameplay, including puzzle progress, settings, stars, and a local copy of the Remove Ads entitlement. This information is stored in the App's local storage on your device; it is not an account or cloud save.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its app data removes local game data. A Remove Ads purchase remains associated with the Apple Account or Google account used to buy it and may be restored through the applicable store.

2. Advertising and consent

The App may show banner and interstitial ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), and may offer rewarded ads for optional in-game bonuses such as a hint or an extra tube. AdMob and its partners may process technical and advertising information such as device and app information, IP address, advertising identifiers where available and permitted, consent signals, and ad interaction or measurement information. Google may use an IP address to infer a general location. Whether ads are personalized depends on your consent, device settings, applicable law, and Google's processing choices.

Google UMP is used where required to request and manage advertising consent. You can review available advertising privacy choices from the App's Privacy options control when it is shown. Gameplay remains available when ads cannot be requested.

Where offered, a one-time Remove Ads purchase disables banner and interstitial ads after the purchase is verified by the applicable store. Rewarded ads may remain available as an optional way to receive bonuses.

3. Purchases

Where the optional non-consumable Remove Ads purchase is offered, Apple processes it through the App Store and StoreKit on iOS, or Google processes it through Google Play Billing on Android. We do not receive or store your payment-card number. The App receives store transaction and product information needed to verify, restore, and apply the Remove Ads entitlement, and stores the resulting entitlement status locally on your device.

4. Analytics and crash reporting

Native builds that have Firebase Analytics configured may send analytics events to Google. These include app-session starts, screen views, the selected in-app language, and events such as starting, completing, or restarting a level, using a hint or extra tube, interacting with rewarded ads, and completing a Remove Ads purchase. These events do not include puzzle boards, solutions, or free-text content. Firebase and Google may also process app-instance identifiers, device and app information, timestamps, and network information needed to provide and measure the service. Firebase Analytics configuration may differ by platform and release build.

The App does not require sign-in and does not send gameplay content to a developer-operated server. It does not use Firebase Crashlytics or Remote Config in the current build.

5. How we use information

  • to save and restore local gameplay and settings;
  • to provide advertising and manage consent when configuration and law allow it;
  • to understand app use through analytics in builds where Firebase Analytics is configured; and
  • to verify and restore the optional Remove Ads purchase through Apple or Google; and
  • to operate, secure, and improve the App.

We do not sell personal information, require an account, or use the App to collect your contacts, photos, messages, microphone data, or precise GPS location. Analytics and advertising data may be associated by the relevant provider with an app installation or device; we do not use the App to identify you by name.

6. Third-party providers

The App may use the following providers, which process information under their own policies and terms:

  • Firebase Analytics: Firebase privacy and security information
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob and UMP: Google partner privacy information
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing: Google Privacy Policy

These providers may process information in countries other than your own.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its app data. Apple, Google, Firebase, and AdMob retain provider-side data according to their applicable policies and retention settings. The App has no user account and does not ask you to enter your name or email address. Analytics or advertising information may still be associated by a provider with an app installation or device. For privacy questions or requests, contact us using the address below; requests involving provider-held data may also need to be handled through that provider.

8. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13, or the higher minimum age required by local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

9. Security

Data sent to the listed providers is transmitted using the security mechanisms provided by those services, including encrypted connections where supported. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App, its SDKs, or its data practices change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
