Privacy Policy for Hashi Bridges

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

Developer: Mladen Stojanovic
Contact: mladenstojanovicpo@gmail.com

Hashi Bridges ("the Game", "we", "us") is a single-player logic-puzzle game. This policy explains the limited data processed when you use the Game and the choices available to you.

Summary

  • The Game does not require an account or sign-in. All puzzle progress, settings, and statistics are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data.
  • We do not collect precise or coarse location data. The Game does not request GPS, network-based, or other location permissions.
  • No puzzle boards, solutions, hints, or notes are sent to any server. What you play stays on your device.
  • Firebase Analytics may be enabled by default where permitted. You can disable it at any time in the Game's Settings under Privacy → Analytics. Where applicable law, regional requirements, or platform rules require prior consent, Analytics remains off until consent is provided. Crashlytics stability diagnostics are a separate system, also controlled in Privacy settings.
  • Core puzzle play continues to work with analytics, crash diagnostics, and all ads disabled. Privacy choices never lock you out of any puzzle.
  • The Game may show advertisements through Google AdMob. A one-time Remove Ads purchase disables those advertisements.
  • Meta is used only for install-attribution measurement (whether a download came from one of our ad campaigns). It is not used to track you across other apps or websites.

1. Information We Process

When the corresponding features are enabled, the Game uses the third-party services listed in Section 3 to process the data described below. None of this is processed by us directly — the services process it under their own privacy policies.

a) Local Data (stored on your device only)

  • Active puzzle state, bridge placements, undo history for the current session
  • Settings, language, theme, audio preferences
  • Tutorial progress and progression through Beginner / Medium bands
  • Per-puzzle records such as best times, No-Hint solves, and Daily Routes history
  • Local statistics such as puzzles solved, Daily Routes solved, No-Hint solves, best times, and recent solve-time statistics.

This data is not transmitted off the device unless you explicitly enable a future cloud-save feature (not present in this release).

b) Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

Hashi Bridges uses Firebase Analytics to understand app usage, puzzle progression, feature engagement, and overall app performance. Where permitted, analytics collection may be enabled by default and can be disabled through the app's Privacy settings. Where applicable law, regional requirements, or platform rules require prior consent, analytics collection remains disabled until consent is provided.

When Analytics is enabled, Firebase Analytics may process:

  • Limited gameplay events, such as starting, completing, or abandoning a puzzle, viewing hint steps, opening screens, and changing settings.
  • Puzzle identifier, difficulty, content version, hint technique used, hint stage reached, elapsed time bucket, undo count, invalid-action count.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating-system version, app version, language, app-instance identifiers, and other technical identifiers used by Firebase services.
  • Approximate location: a coarse region derived from a masked IP address by Firebase Analytics for aggregated reporting. The Game does not request or collect GPS or other precise location data.

We do not set a Firebase user ID. We do not send puzzle boards, solutions, hint copy, notes, or any other player-created puzzle content to Firebase.

Separation from advertising and tracking. Advertising, personalized advertising, Meta advertising measurement, and other tracking-related processing are controlled separately and may require additional consent. Accepting or rejecting ads does not automatically enable or disable Analytics, and Analytics is not used for advertising, marketing, or personalization.

c) Diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)

Firebase Crashlytics is a separate stability-diagnostics system. It is not used for advertising, marketing, personalization, or tracking, and its consent is independent of advertising consent. When Crashlytics is enabled, it may process:

  • Crash logs: stack traces, relevant app state at the time of the crash, and a Firebase installation UUID used to dedupe and prioritize stability issues.
  • Device and app metadata that Firebase Crashlytics needs to deliver and group crash reports.

d) Advertising (only when enabled)

When ads are enabled, the Game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to load and display ads at selected non-solving boundaries (between puzzles, after a Result, in free-play interstitials). Google may process information such as the device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, approximate location inferred from the IP, and ad interaction data to provide, measure, secure, and personalize its advertising services. Whether an ad is personalized depends on your device, your Google account, applicable law, and the consent choice you make in the Game's Privacy Options screen (managed by Google UMP, see Section 3).

e) Install Attribution (Meta)

The Game uses Meta's install-attribution tools to measure whether a download came from one of our advertising campaigns on Meta platforms. Meta may process the Android Advertising ID, install referrer information, and campaign or click identifiers. This data is used solely for measuring install sources and is not used by us for cross-app tracking-based advertising or to build a profile of you across other apps or websites.

f) In-App Purchase (Remove Ads)

You can purchase Remove Ads as a one-time in-app product through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the transaction and the purchase record. The Game receives only the information needed to verify, restore, and apply the entitlement (purchase state, product identifier, and the receipt validation token). We do not receive or store your payment-card details, billing address, or other payment credentials.

g) Support Communications

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and the message content. We use it only to respond to your request.

2. How We Use Data

  • Operate the Game and remember your progress on your device.
  • Understand how the Game performs, identify crashes, prioritize fixes, and improve stability, balance, and onboarding.
  • Measure whether our ad campaigns lead to installs (Meta attribution) and how the Game's advertising performs (AdMob).
  • Process and restore the optional Remove Ads purchase through Google Play Billing.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to valid privacy requests.

We do not use Firebase analytics data for advertising, marketing, personalization, or account management.

4. Your Choices and Controls

The Game provides an in-app Settings → Privacy area with the following controls:

  • Privacy Policy — opens this page.
  • Privacy Options — opens the Google UMP consent flow so you can revisit or change your EEA / UK / Switzerland ad-consent choice at any time. The Game does not hide this control when UMP reports that it is required.
  • Analytics — shows the current state of Firebase Analytics collection and lets you turn product analytics on or off. Analytics is enabled by default where permitted; where prior consent is required by law or platform rules, Analytics remains off until consent is provided. Analytics consent is separate from advertising consent and from Crashlytics diagnostics consent.
  • Diagnostics — shows the current state of Firebase Crashlytics stability-diagnostics collection and lets you turn it on or off. Diagnostics consent is separate from advertising consent and from Analytics consent.
  • Ad personalization — surfaces the current ad-consent choice and lets you open Privacy Options to change it.
  • Reset local progress — clears puzzle progress, statistics, and settings from your device. This is a device-side reset, not a request to delete vendor-side data; vendor-side controls are listed below.

In addition, you can:

  • Reset or limit the Android Advertising ID at any time from your device's Google settings.
  • Uninstall the Game or clear its app data to remove all locally stored puzzle progress, settings, and statistics from your device.
  • Use platform-level controls to manage Google ad personalization and Firebase data.
  • Contact us for any other privacy question or request (see Section 12).

Core puzzle play, including the First-Time User Experience, free play, the Daily Route, the Smart Hint system, the Result screen, and local Statistics, continues to work with analytics, crash diagnostics, and ads disabled. Privacy choices never remove or lock any puzzle.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its app data.
  • Firebase retains diagnostic and analytics data according to Google Firebase's applicable retention settings and policies.
  • Google retains AdMob and UMP data according to its own policies and current legal requirements.
  • Meta retains install-attribution data according to its own policies.
  • Google Play retains the Remove Ads purchase record so the entitlement can be restored on your Google Play account.

Because the Game has no user account and does not associate Firebase data with your name, email address, or other direct identifier, we cannot reliably locate or delete anonymous Firebase records for a particular player on request. For any privacy question you can contact us at mladenstojanovicpo@gmail.com.

6. Sale and Sharing

We do not sell personal information for money. We share data only with the service providers listed in Section 3, and only as needed to operate the Game, process the optional Remove Ads purchase, deliver and measure ads, attribute installs, and provide analytics or crash diagnostics.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is designed for a general puzzle-playing audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the Game, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to address the issue.

8. Data Security

All third-party services used by the Game transmit data over encrypted connections (TLS). We configure event parameters to exclude personal information and to limit the diagnostic data we attach to crashes. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the limited data the Game processes.

9. International Data Transfers

Firebase, AdMob, UMP, Meta, and Google Play data may be processed by their providers in countries other than your own, subject to those providers' own policies and applicable law (including the European Union's standard contractual clauses and equivalent transfer mechanisms).

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California, or other regions with privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal data, to object to certain processing, and to data portability. To make a request, contact us at mladenstojanovicpo@gmail.com. For data held directly by Google or Meta, use the controls those providers offer.
