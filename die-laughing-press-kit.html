STORY (ONE PARAGRAPH)

Die Laughing is a roguelite wave survival game built for mobile by solo developer Mladen Stojanović. The player is a grinning skull on a round white ball body with no arms and no legs, surviving 7 escalating waves of enemies using up to 17 weapons thrown via telekinesis. Tone is sarcastic horror: every death is funny until it isn't, and every run is different. The tagline is SURVIVE. DIE. REPEAT.
